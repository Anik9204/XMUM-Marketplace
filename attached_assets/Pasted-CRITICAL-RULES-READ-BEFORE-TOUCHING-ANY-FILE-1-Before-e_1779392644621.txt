## ISSUE — Search tab: price filter shows no results unless a keyword is also typed

FILE: src/pages/SearchPage.tsx

  if (!keyword.trim() && !searched) return;

   const hasActiveFilter = !!minPrice || !!maxPrice || (condition !== "all");
