# XMUM Market — Remove Order System & Review System

## Context & Purpose

XMUM Market is being repositioned from a transaction platform (like Shopee) to a
**listings directory and contact connector** (like Mudah.my). Buyers browse listings,
find contact info, and reach out to sellers directly. The app does NOT process
transactions. Therefore:

- The **Order System** must be removed entirely.
- The **Review System** must be removed entirely.
- The **Inquiry System** must be KEPT — it is the primary "contact seller" mechanism.
- In-app **Messaging** must be KEPT — it is a core feature.
- All other features remain untouched.
