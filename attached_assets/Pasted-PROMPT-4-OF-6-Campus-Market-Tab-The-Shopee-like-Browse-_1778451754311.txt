### STEP 1 — Create `src/pages/CampusMarketPage.tsx`
 
This is the central discovery page for Campus Market — think mini-Shopee. It should feel vibrant and polished.
 
**Layout (mobile-first):**
 
1. **Hero banner area** at the top:
   - Rotating display of approved `ShopAd` records (from `getApprovedShopAds()`)
   - If no ads: show a static "Campus Market — Shop from fellow XMUM students" hero banner with brand color gradient
   - Auto-rotates every 5 seconds
2. **Category filter row** — horizontal scrollable chips:
   - "All", then each `ShopCategory` value
   - Active category is highlighted with brand color `#003366`
3. **Featured Shops row** — horizontal scroll of shop cards:
   - Shows all active shops (from `getAllShops()`)
   - Each shop card: logo/avatar, shop name, category, star rating
   - Clicking → navigates to `/shop/[slug]`
4. **Listings grid** — masonry or 2-column grid:
   - Shows `ShopListing` items filtered by selected category
   - Each card: photo (or placeholder), listing title, price + pricing model, shop name
   - Clicking a listing card → navigates to `/shop/[slug]` (the public shop page handles the detail)
   - "Load more" button at the bottom
5. **"Open Your Shop" floating CTA** at the bottom (mobile) or sidebar (desktop):
   - Button: "🏪 Open Your Shop" → navigates to `/create-shop`
**Data loading:**
- Use `getAllShops()` for shops
- Use `getAllShopListings()` for listings (default), `getShopListingsByCategory()` when category selected
- Show skeleton loaders while loading
- Show "No listings yet — be the first to open a shop!" empty state
**Design requirements:**
- Mobile-first, full dark mode support
- Brand color `#003366` for accents and active states
- Category chips are horizontally scrollable with hidden scrollbar
- Clean card shadows, rounded corners (rounded-2xl)
- Smooth tab/filter transitions
