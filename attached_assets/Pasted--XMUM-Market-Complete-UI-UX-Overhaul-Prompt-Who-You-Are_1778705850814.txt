# XMUM Market — Complete UI/UX Overhaul Prompt

## Who You Are & What You're Doing

You are a senior UI/UX frontend engineer working on XMUM Market, a community
marketplace PWA for Xiamen University Malaysia students. The app is built in
React 19 + Vite + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS 4, using Wouter for routing and
Firebase as the backend.

You are performing a **complete visual and interaction overhaul** of the student
app (`artifacts/xmum-market`). The goal is to make it feel like a polished,
modern mobile-native app — on par with Carousell and Mudah.my — that specifically
resonates with university students aged 18–25.

**Zero functional logic will be changed.** All data fetching, Firebase calls,
state management, routing, auth, and business rules stay exactly as they are.
You are only changing visual presentation, layout, spacing, typography, colours,
component styling, and micro-interactions.

## Design Direction & Principles

### Target Audience
University students aged 18–25, primarily on Android and iOS mobile browsers.
Many are international students (Malaysian, Chinese). They are comfortable with
apps like Shopee, Carousell, Xiaohongshu, and WhatsApp. They expect:
- Clean, modern visuals that feel current and trustworthy
- Fast, thumb-friendly navigation
- Clear hierarchy — they should never wonder "what does this button do?"
- Friendly, not corporate — energy and personality over formality

### Design Reference
- **Carousell** (mobile listing cards, bottom nav, clean product pages)
- **Mudah.my** (local feel, practical layout, contact-focused)
- **Shopee** (for Campus Market only — vibrant, category chips, card grids)

### Aesthetic Direction: Refined Student Energy
- **Colour palette**: Keep the existing XMUM brand navy `#003366` as the primary
  accent. Pair it with a bright, friendly secondary accent `#0EA5E9` (sky blue)
  for highlights, active states, and badges. Use warm white `#F8FAFC` for
  backgrounds (already in place). Use `#1E293B` for primary text (not pure black).
  Keep existing dark mode slate palette.
- **Typography**: The app currently uses Inter. **Add Nunito as the display/heading
  font** for section headers, card titles, tab labels, and the app name — it reads
  as friendly and modern without being childish. Keep Inter for body copy, inputs,
  and small UI labels. Import Nunito via Google Fonts in `index.css`.
- **Corner radius**: Use `rounded-2xl` (16px) as the standard card radius. Use
  `rounded-xl` (12px) for buttons, inputs, and chips. Use `rounded-full` for
  badges, pills, and avatars.
- **Shadows**: Use a consistent soft shadow system — `shadow-sm` for cards at
  rest, `shadow-md` for cards on hover, `shadow-xl` for modals and bottom sheets.
  Avoid hard/dark shadows.
- **Spacing**: Generous padding inside cards. `px-4 py-4` minimum for card
  content. `gap-3` minimum between list items.
- **Motion**: Add `transition-all duration-200` on interactive elements.
  Cards get `hover:scale-[1.02] active:scale-[0.98]`. Buttons get
  `hover:brightness-110 active:brightness-90` instead of colour shifts where
  possible. Keep existing animations where they exist.
