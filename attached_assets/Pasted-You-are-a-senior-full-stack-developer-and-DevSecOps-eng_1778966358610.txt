className, children all stay identical). Do NOT change anything else
in VerificationPage.tsx.

FIX 4 — UsersPage: role <select> click bubbles to row, opens drawer
File: artifacts/xmum-admin/src/pages/UsersPage.tsx
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

DIAGNOSIS:
The <tr> has onClick={() => openDrawer(u)}. The role <select> inside it
has no stopPropagation. In many browsers, clicking or interacting with a
<select> inside a clickable <tr> fires the row's onClick as well, which
calls openDrawer simultaneously with handleRoleChange — the drawer opens
unexpectedly every time the admin touches the role dropdown.

SURGICAL EDIT — add one prop to the <select> element:

Find the <select> for role change in the table (around line 223-224):
  <select
    value={u.role || "user"}
    onChange={e => handleRoleChange(u, e.target.value as UserRole)}

Add onClick={e => e.stopPropagation()} so it reads:
  <select
    value={u.role || "user"}
    onChange={e => handleRoleChange(u, e.target.value as UserRole)}
    onClick={e => e.stopPropagation()}

Do NOT change value, onChange, className, or the <option> children.
Do NOT change anything else in UsersPage.tsx in this step.
